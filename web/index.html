UK take-home pay, 2026-27

The figures on this page are computed in your browser by uktax, the Lean model the repository's proofs are about, compiled to WebAssembly; the two worked out on the page itself are labelled. The proofs cover stated properties of the model (for example, that income tax never falls as income rises, and every pay at which take-home can fall); they do not prove that it follows the law, which the hand-worked golden cases and the comparison with PolicyEngine test. Nothing is sent to a server.

What this covers, and what it doesn't

The model covers income tax (UK and Scottish rates), employee National Insurance, the High Income Child Benefit Charge, Child Benefit, Universal Credit, Tax-Free Childcare and funded childcare hours for 2026-27 (or for the figures you enter), and more than this page asks for (see the repository README).

  • Out of scope on this page: other benefits the model has (PIP, Carer's Allowance, Pension Credit, Housing Benefit, Council Tax Reduction…), self-employment, property income, capital gains, Stamp Duty.
  • Not in the model: every tax year other than 2026-27, and the items under "Out of scope" in the README. The model grows faster than this page: the README is the full list of what it covers.
  • Childcare support follows England's rules wherever you live. Universal Credit here is a single claimant or a couple where the partner has no pay, with pay as the only income.
  • Take-home counts the larger of Universal Credit and Tax-Free Childcare (they cannot be had together), plus funded hours at their value.
  • This is a prototype for trying the compiled model. It is not advice.
